# LCAI-0012B — Diagnostic and Remediation Audit

## Fractions

- Misconception: adding numerators and denominators independently.
- Diagnostic: three fraction additions classify answers and error patterns.
- Remediation: explicitly analyzes the false `1/3 + 1/4 = 2/7` method,
  rebuilds a common denominator and contrasts equal denominators.
- Result: coherent targeted pair.

## Distributivity

- Misconception: multiplying only the first term in parentheses.
- Diagnostic: expand a three-term expression.
- Remediation: analyzes `4(x + 2y) = 4x + 2y`, applies multiplication to
  every term, states the general rule and transfers it.
- Result: coherent targeted pair.

## Pythagoras

- Misconception: adding side lengths instead of squares.
- Diagnostic: direct hypotenuse calculation in a known right triangle.
- Remediation: critiques `BC = AB + AC`, states the squared relation and
  recalculates the 3-4-5 case.
- Result: coherent targeted pair.

The remediation prompts do more than relabel practice: each names or exposes a
specific faulty strategy, explains why it fails and requests a corrected
method. The paired families remain Draft and require human editorial review.
