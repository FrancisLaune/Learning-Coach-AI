# LCAI-0013B — DNB 2024–2026 Multi-Subject Block V1

## Scope
This block introduces a shared DNB preparation structure for:
- French
- History-Geography-EMC
- Sciences

Years covered at source/inventory level: 2024, 2025 and 2026.

## 2026 first decomposition
The 2026 official examination catalogue exposes multiple geographical sessions and, for the main zones, separate official PDFs for French, HG-EMC and Sciences.

### French
Modelled as distinct components:
- grammar / language skills / comprehension / interpretation;
- dictation;
- writing.

The 2026 framework gives French coefficient 2 and 3 hours overall. The writing component is 40 points and 1 h 30; dictation is 10 points and 20 minutes.

### History-Geography-EMC
Modelled around:
- document analysis;
- constructed response and historical/geographical landmarks;
- EMC civic reasoning.

Document and annex dependencies are retained explicitly.

### Sciences
Modelled as a one-hour, coefficient-2 paper involving two scientific disciplines on the same script. Scientific documents and experimental evidence are explicit dependencies.

## 2024–2025 historical-format metadata
The block also records the official 2024 and 2025 scoring framework so the application does not confuse older papers with the 2026 reform.

2024/2025 terminal written scoring:
- French: 100 points
- Mathematics: 100 points
- HG-EMC: 50 points
- Sciences: 50 points

From 2025, HG and EMC marks are separated on the transcript although the examination remains a single paper.

## Integration rule
No exact LCAI Skill ID is invented in this block. `skill_hints` must later be resolved against the authoritative LCAI-0011C 3e curriculum.

## Copyright/data rule
Store source provenance, structure, mappings and short descriptors. Do not copy full official literary texts, document corpora or complete papers into the normalized Learning Coach dataset.
