# LCAI-0012A — Difficulty Model

Difficulty is relative to the grade, chapter and target Skill. It must not be
used to introduce content above the target curriculum.

| Level | Meaning | Typical autonomy |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Foundation | Direct application, one concept, familiar representation | Guided |
| 2 — Standard | Expected grade-level application and moderate reasoning | Normal |
| 3 — Advanced | Multi-step reasoning, transfer or combination | Independent |

The profile is interpreted by discipline:

- Mathematics: number and representation complexity, operations, steps,
  method choice and transfer.
- French: text length, syntax, implicit information, language phenomena and
  production constraints.
- Modern languages: vocabulary/grammar range, message length, contextual
  support and production autonomy.
- History, geography and EMC: source complexity, contextualisation,
  argumentation and cross-document reasoning.
- Sciences: data/model complexity, calculation, experimental reasoning and
  multi-step inference.

Anti-patterns include equating level 3 with above-grade knowledge, inflating
numbers without changing reasoning, applying mathematics dimensions to every
subject, or labelling direct recall “advanced”. Deterministic checks reject
levels outside 1–3; uncertain semantic mismatches remain reviewer warnings.
