# LCAI-0012B — Difficulty Audit

Five Mathematics Skills received explicit level comparisons.

| Skill | Difficulty 1 | Difficulty 2 | Difficulty 3 | Judgment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fraction addition | Two direct additions with shown steps | Three-term mixed operation | Difference of two grouped sums | Genuine operation/organization increase |
| Distributivity | Direct single and double products | Required term-by-term justification and reduction | Nested brackets and several signed distributions | Genuine structural complexity |
| Pythagoras | Direct 6-8-10 diagnostic | Contextual length/reverse checks | Radicals and two configurations with theorem choice | Genuine representation and method increase |
| Proportionality | One unit-rate application | Table, formula and time conversion | Multi-part modelling and proof of proportionality | Genuine autonomy and modelling increase |
| First-degree equations | Direct equation | Translate prose, solve and verify | Successive percentage modelling before solving | Genuine transfer and multi-step reasoning |

The progression is not based solely on larger numbers. It increases the
number of concepts coordinated, representation changes, method selection,
justification and autonomy. All examples remain within the declared grade.

One SVT remediation candidate requiring a logarithmic magnitude calculation
was rejected manually as above the intended 4e scope. This demonstrates that
structural validation alone is not sufficient for difficulty alignment.

**Answer:** difficulty increases primarily through reasoning complexity,
representation and autonomy, not superficial numeric inflation.
